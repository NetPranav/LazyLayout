# Task 3D-008 Fix 1 Handoff

## 1. `invertAffine()` in `src/core/3d/math.ts`
- **What was wrong:** The 3x3 block inversion had rows and columns transposed in its cofactor assignment (`res[1]` assigned $C_{10}$ instead of $C_{01}$), which diverged from general `invert()` when the scale was non-uniform.
- **What was changed:** Recomputed the full 3x3 inverse matrix via adjugate $B_{i,j} = C_{j,i}/\det$ in column-major order and translation as $-A^{-1} t$, restoring exact numerical parity between `invertAffine()` and `invert()`.

## 2. `setOriginToCenterOfMass` on closed cube in `src/core/3d/__tests__/apply.test.ts`
- **What was wrong:** The signed-tetrahedra algorithm in `apply.ts` was mathematically sound, but `createSampleCubeMesh()` in the unit test had its Top (+Y) face wound clockwise (`3, 2, 6,  3, 6, 7`, inward normal $[0, -4, 0]$), causing top-face tetrahedra to subtract volume and corrupt the centroid calculation.
- **What was changed:** Fixed Top face winding to counter-clockwise (`3, 6, 2,  3, 7, 6`, outward normal $[0, +4, 0]$); the closed cube now integrates consistently to volume +8 and centers exactly at $[5, -3, 2]$, satisfying `positions[0] === -1` and `positions[18] === +1`.
