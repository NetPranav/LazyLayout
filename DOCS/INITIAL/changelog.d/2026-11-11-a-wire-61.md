## Phase 61 in the new editor: the timeline's edits are the document's

- The work area set from the playhead (I / O buttons) is saved in the composition and undone in one step; before, it only moved the clock.
- `tests/e2e/wire-61-timeline.spec.ts`: a ten-step journey in the new timeline (trim an animation's end and start, move one, set the work area, add a marker, solo, lock, undo twice), checking the document after each step; solo hides the other layers on the canvas.

### Fixed
- A press that began on a layer no longer deselects it when the layer moves from under the pointer before the release (dragging while recording keys, or a layer animating under it); only a press on the background itself deselects.
