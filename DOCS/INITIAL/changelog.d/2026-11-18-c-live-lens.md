### Added

- **Lens on a Live fill: "magnify what's under it".** Turn on **Lens** on a Live fill and the source is drawn lined up under the shape, magnified about its centre by **Magnify**. It stays lined up however the shape moves: dragged, keyframed, following the pointer, or scrolled. So any shape can be a magnifier or a glass bubble. Put a solid fill below the lens fill to hide what's behind it. It is checked to the pixel (0 pixels differ at 100%), and it runs only while a lens is on the page.
