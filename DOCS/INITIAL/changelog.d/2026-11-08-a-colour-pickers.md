## Colour pickers everywhere

- The colour squares of the Page background, the Theme and Tokens colours (Primary, Surface, Text) and the Icon, Toggle, Background and 3D object colours were plain squares. They now open the editor's colour picker (saturation and brightness square, hue bar, hex), like Fill and Stroke.
- The picker has an **eyedropper** button (where the browser provides one, such as Chrome and Edge): click it, then click anywhere on screen to take that colour.
