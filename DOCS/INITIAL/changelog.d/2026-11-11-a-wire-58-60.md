## Phases 58 to 60 in the new editor: Web Animations, scrub versus play, and the parity badge

- 58 and 59 came with the engine (Phase 57): while playing, routed animations run as Web Animations on the compositor; while scrubbing or paused, the kernel draws. Paused after playing, every layer shows the kernel's value.
- 60: the engine parity badge is in the timeline footer (dev builds and tests only): idle, "Parity OK" while the engines match `evaluate()`, "Parity drift" when they don't, with what differs in its tooltip. It is a design-system component (`ParityBadge`) with a demo of its three states.
- The canvas test seam `window.__LL_ENGINE__` gains `time`, `loop`, `parity` and `document`, as the old stage had.
- `tests/e2e/wire-58-60-engines.spec.ts`: Web Animations while playing and not while scrubbing; pause stops them; paused after playing matches the kernel at five times; correct playback stays green and an injected wrong easing raises `[PARITY_DRIFT]` and turns the badge red.
