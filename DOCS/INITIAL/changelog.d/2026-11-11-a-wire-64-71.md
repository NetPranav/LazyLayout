## Phases 64 to 71 in the new editor

- **64 · Timeline at scale.** A scrub re-renders only the open lanes (the others don't depend on the playhead). Lanes are memoized. With more than 40 layers, rows out of view are not built: an empty block of the same height stands in for each.
- **65 · Composition tabs.** A tab strip above the timeline (Main, each interaction composition with its trigger, each opened precomp with a ×), **+ Precomp**, and breadcrumbs. Precomps placed in the composition show as rows with their keys; double-click or **Open** opens them. The lanes, the edits, the motion path and onion skin all follow the composition shown.
- **66 · Keyframing.** **Distribute evenly**, **Slower** and **Faster** on the selected keyframes, each one undo step.
- **67 · Graph and easing.** The graph draws the engine's curves (the kernel's easing maths) as a **Value** or a **Speed** graph. A **Feel** submenu on a keyframe holds Snappy, Smooth, Bouncy and Lazy (springs by Bounce and Time), the curves and Hold. Easings are saved as real strings: curves keep their handles and springs their Bounce and Time. "Back out" was saved as `back-out`, which isn't a valid easing; it's now `back.out`. The keyframes selected in the timeline are the engine's selection, so F9 (Easy Ease) and Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V work on them.
- **68 · One animation source.** The code view and the export emit the document's clips (what the timeline makes) as GSAP hooks, not only the legacy `animationSamples` (`src/core/animation/clipSamples.ts`).
- **69–71.** The motion path, the Value HUD and onion skin were already on the new canvas. The motion path and onion skin now follow the composition shown.
