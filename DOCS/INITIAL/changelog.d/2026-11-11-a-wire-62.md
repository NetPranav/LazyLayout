## Phase 62 in the new editor: twirl-downs and stopwatches

- Each layer in the timeline has a Properties twirl: Transform (position, scale, rotation, skew) and Appearance (opacity, corner radius, fill and border colour, border width) list every property in plain words with a stopwatch; Effects, States, Behaviours and Links list what the layer has.
- A stopwatch turned on puts one keyframe at the playhead holding the current value (one undo step); turned off it removes the property's keyframes. It survives reload.
- U reveals the animated properties of the selected layers, U twice the modified ones (the engine's own commands, now shown in the new timeline).
