## Smart cutout: snap to edges, depth

- **Snap to edges** (Cut out, on by default): a shape you draw settles onto the real edge of the object within Reach of your line, the way a phone's smart select does. Cut works the same, so what you remove is removed along the edge. Pixels deep inside the shape are taken as sure foreground and pixels well outside as sure background; a colour model of each and the picture's own edges decide the band between, and the edge is softened along the picture's own edges (a guided filter), not blurred.
- **Depth**: the picture's depth is estimated from haze (the dark channel method), height in the frame and detail. "Show depth" colours the picture cool (far) to warm (near). A new **Depth** way to pick selects everything at about the depth you click (Depth range sets how much), snapped to edges. **Depth layers** adds aligned copies of the picture on top of it, each showing only what is nearer than a depth, so something placed between two of them is hidden by the nearer one.
- The depth is an estimate for landscapes and hazy scenes, not true 3D. A neural depth model would be more exact on other pictures, but it needs a new dependency and a model download, so it is not part of this.
- New pure module `src/core/image/smart.ts` with unit tests; `CutoutDialog` gets the controls.
