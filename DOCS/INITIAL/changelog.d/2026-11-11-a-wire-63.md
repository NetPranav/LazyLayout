## Phase 63 in the new editor: After Effects keys in the timeline

- With the pointer over the timeline: Page Down / Page Up step a frame (Shift: ten), I and O jump to the selected layers' in and out points (the work area's ends with nothing selected), B and N set the work area from the playhead (saved), = and - zoom by 1.25, \ fits the work area, Home and End go to the start and the end. These are the engine's timeline commands; the panel no longer handles them itself.
- The timeline's zoom and the engine's zoom are one value: the panel reports its width and follows the engine's zoom and scroll requests.
- The work-area buttons in the toolbar say B and N, matching the keys.

### Fixed
- Space over the focused timeline toggled playback twice (so nothing happened), and an arrow stepped two frames: a key the panel had handled was run again by the command bus. The command bus now skips keys already handled.
- After clicking the Design / Motion / Code tabs, Home, End and the arrows switched workspace instead of reaching the timeline. The tabs now move with those keys only when they were reached with the keyboard.
- `tests/e2e/wire-63-navigation.spec.ts`: the keyboard-only journey lands on exact times.
