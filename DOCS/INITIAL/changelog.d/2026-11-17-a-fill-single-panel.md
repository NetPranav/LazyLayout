### Changed

- **A fill has one panel, as in Figma's fill picker.** The fill row's settings button (the sliders icon) and its second popup are gone. The swatch or the colour field opens one panel beside the inspector:
  - The kinds of fill are icons along its top: Solid, Gradient, Image, Pattern, Noise, Mesh and Live.
  - Gradient then offers Linear, Radial or Conic.
  - Each kind's own options are in that panel: gradient stops by number, the picture's Cut out and adjustments, the pattern, noise and live settings, and the mesh grid.
  - A "Blend & mask" group under them applies to every kind.
  - A layer that takes one plain colour (text, buttons) shows the Solid tab only.
- A picture dropped on the panel's image area now becomes the fill. Before, it was only previewed.
