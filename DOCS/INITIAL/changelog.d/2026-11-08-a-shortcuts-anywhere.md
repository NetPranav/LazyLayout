## Delete, Backspace and Select All work with the pointer anywhere

- Backspace (the Mac delete key) now deletes the selection like Delete.
- Select All, Delete, Backspace, copy, paste, duplicate, group and the other selection shortcuts used to be ignored while the pointer was over the inspector or the bars; they now work anywhere except over the timeline and the blueprint, and never while a text field has focus. The arrow-key nudge keeps its stricter scope so it cannot fire while moving through a dropdown.
