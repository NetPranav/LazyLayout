## Decisions made
- Kept option popovers at 280px width, 36px header (`.llm-h`) with sans 11px/500 muted title and 24px close button, 72px label column (`.dlbl`), and 28px fields with hairline outlines matching P lines 553–564 and `<script id="ll-more">`.
- Built separate components per section in `src/editor/panels/details/options/` rather than jamming all controls into one bloated file, allowing clean per-section props and isolation.
- Both swatch click and hex input click open the ColorPicker across Fill, Stroke, Page background, and Theme & Tokens colors, satisfying Item 2.
- ColorPicker and OptionsPopover track the scroll container `.ll-zbody` and close if scrolled out of the visible inspector viewport, matching P lines 2495–2500 and 12007.
- Rows of collapsed group nodes remain in the DOM with `.lcl` (`display: none`), allowing WAAPI height/opacity/scale/blur spring animations to run seamlessly on fold and unfold.
